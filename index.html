How does the matching survey work?+

Your answers are compared with the sample provider profiles shown here to suggest a possible fit. It’s a simple starting point, not a clinical assessment, and your answers are not stored or sent anywhere.

Are these real providers?+

No. The names and profiles on this prototype are examples only. A real service should replace them with verified provider information, credentials, locations, availability, and contact details.

What if I’m not sure what kind of support I need?+

That’s okay. You don’t need to have the right words or a clear plan before reaching out. You can choose “I’m open to suggestions” in the survey and use the result as a conversation starter.

Can I use this in a crisis?+

This website is not crisis support and is not monitored. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services or a crisis line in your country.
